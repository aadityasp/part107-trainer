Part 107 Trainer

FAA remote pilot knowledge test prep. Free, offline, cited.

Preparation for the FAA Part 107 examination, the test required to operate a small unmanned aircraft commercially in the United States. Built on one principle. You should never have to take our word for anything.

Every answer cites its source

After you answer, you get an explanation together with the exact citation, such as 14 CFR 107.51(c). You can confirm it against the regulation yourself in seconds. If an answer here is ever wrong, you will be able to prove it.

Sectional chart figures that work

Chart reading is the most demanding part of the exam and where study apps most often fail. Official FAA testing figures are bundled inside the app. They open instantly, zoom for fine detail, and work with no network connection. If a figure cannot be displayed, the app says so rather than showing an empty frame.

Dispute any question

Every question carries a dispute control. Disputed questions are visibly marked as under review for everyone, with the source shown, rather than being quietly withdrawn or quietly left in place.
