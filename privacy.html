Privacy Policy

Part 107 Trainer · Last updated 4 August 2026

Part 107 Trainer does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data. There is no account, no sign-up, and the app requires no internet connection to function.

What is stored

Your practice progress — which questions you have answered, your accuracy per topic, and your queue of missed questions — is stored only in the app's local storage on your own device. It never leaves the device. Deleting the app deletes it.

What is not collected

No analytics, no advertising identifiers, no crash reporting, no location data, no contacts, no device identifiers, and no third-party trackers of any kind. The app contains no advertising and no in-app purchases.

Disputed questions

If you use the dispute button to report a question, the report is saved on your device. It is only sent anywhere if you explicitly choose to share it using your device's share sheet, and you can see exactly what it contains before you send it. A report includes the question identifier, the answer the app gave, the content version, and any note you write. It does not include anything that identifies you.

Children

The app is a study aid for an adult professional certification and is not directed at children. Because it collects no data at all, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date. Since the app collects nothing, any change would be to clarify rather than to expand collection.
