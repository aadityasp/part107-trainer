Part 107 Trainer

Privacy Policy

Part 107 Trainer. Last updated October 10, 2026.

Part 107 Trainer works offline, has no account, and does not collect personal data. There are no ads, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind. The only network request the app makes is a check for content corrections, described below.

What stays on your device

Your practice progress, meaning which questions you have answered, your accuracy per topic, and your queue of missed questions, is stored only in the app's local storage on your own device. It never leaves the device. Deleting the app deletes it.

What is not collected

No analytics, no advertising identifiers, no crash reporting, no location data, no contacts, no device identifiers, and no third-party trackers. There is no sign-up and no login.

Content updates

Every question and chart is bundled inside the app, so studying never needs a connection. When the app opens and a connection is available, it checks Expo's update server for content corrections, so a fix can reach you without waiting for an App Store or Google Play release. This check is made by expo-updates, a library from Expo. The request tells Expo's server the app's runtime version, your platform (iOS or Android), the update channel, and the identifier of the update currently installed on your device. As with any internet request, Expo's servers also see your IP address. We add no account, device, or advertising identifier to the request, and we do not link it to you. How Expo handles the request is described in Expo's privacy policy. If you are offline, the app skips the check and keeps working.

Price and purchases

Part 107 Trainer is free. Every feature is included, and there are no in-app purchases, no subscriptions and no ads. The app takes no payment and never asks for payment details. If that ever changes, this policy will be updated before the release that changes it.

Beta testing

If you test a pre-release version through Apple's TestFlight or Google Play testing, those services collect their own information about testers, for example your email address and any feedback or crash data you choose to send, under Apple's and Google's privacy policies. We see only what those services show the developer, plus anything you choose to send us.

Disputed questions

If you use the dispute button to report a question, the report is saved on your device. It is only sent anywhere if you explicitly choose to share it using your device's share sheet, and you can see exactly what it contains before you send it. A report includes the question identifier, the answer the app gave, the content version, and any note you write. It does not include anything that identifies you.

Children

The app is a study aid for an adult professional certification and is not directed at children. Because it collects no personal data, it collects none from children either.
